# Measurement and observer tools review: fixed-camera 2D path drift

**Date:** 2026-10-02  
**Scope:** measurement lane for the 17-frame Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B M0 fixed-camera red-ball pilot. This is a read-only research deliverable: no code was changed and no tracker, segmenter, model, or cloud runtime was run.

## Recommendation

Use **blinded human boxes as ground truth**, with SAM 2.1 (tiny/small) as an optional first-frame-prompted mask proposal. Use TAPNext++ only as an independent point-motion diagnostic and RAFT only to inspect coherent background motion. Keep OWLv2 as an optional framewise detection proposal/comparability check. Do not use any one pretrained observer as the evaluator or call its score a calibrated confidence.

The current 0.65 red-mask stability threshold is a heuristic. Freeze a threshold only after comparing its risk and coverage with human labels on development clips; then pass it unchanged on new-seed clips. If it fails, the first repair should be a separately validated object mask/track observer, not a looser threshold chosen on the held-out samples.

The current context is a 832×480, 17-frame clip with controller observations at frames `[0,4,8,12,16]` on a 52×30 DiTraj spatial grid. One grid cell is 16×16 pixels. The existing Kubric truth fixture validates scorer arithmetic and path sign; it does not validate a machine observer on Wan previews. Current human double-label reliability is still pending in the workspace notes.

### What published video-control scores do and do not establish

DiTraj's paper uses OWL-ViT-large detections, then reports coverage, mean box IoU, center distance, and AP50. Its coverage counts clips whose detector boxes exist for more than half the frames. That is useful secondary comparability, but it is detector-dependent and does not itself establish that one identity persisted or that a target did not split into duplicates. Keep those metrics alongside, not in place of, manually labeled visibility, identity, and center error. [DiTraj paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.21839), [official implementation](https://github.com/xduzhangjiayu/DiTraj).

TAP-Vid supplies human-annotated point tracks for real videos and exact tracks for synthetic videos; its task asks a tracker to predict point location and visibility. It supports the use of visibility-aware point-tracking diagnostics, but a surface point track must not be treated as an object's center. [TAP-Vid](https://tapvid.github.io/), [official TAP repository](https://github.com/google-deepmind/tapnet).

## Label contract and observer validation

### Annotation unit and blinding

1. The label unit is a rendered **17-frame stream**. Annotators see frame indices and within-clip order (needed for identity), but clip IDs are randomized and they do not see the method arm, seed, requested left/right path, observer output, confidence, or each other's labels.
2. Label the exact decoded stream that the policy reads. If the intervention reads step-4 predicted-clean output, manually label that step-4 `x0_hat` stream. Label step-14 independently if it will drive a separate update. Do not treat step-4 and step-14 outputs as adjacent frames or pool them as independent videos. Label final rendered output separately for the outcome score.
3. For every frame record: visible / not visible / ambiguous; count of instances matching the described target; target identity continuous / uncertain / changed; tight visible-object box `[x1,y1,x2,y2]`; occlusion state; notes. Exclude shadow and detached specular glare; include the visible object silhouette. For a partially visible but identifiable object, box the visible silhouette and mark partial. For fully absent or ambiguous identity, do not invent a center.
4. Define center as the **tight visible bounding-box midpoint** for both humans and machine proposals, matching `path_annotation_scoring.py`. If later tasks need a physical sphere center under occlusion or an asymmetric object's centroid/orientation, add segmentation masks or keypoints as a separate annotation target; do not silently change this metric.
5. SAM2 may propose a mask after a human initial prompt, but annotators must be able to correct it and the resulting manual labels are still the reference. Do not initialize a tracker with a hidden ground-truth box and then claim end-to-end detection or correct first-frame placement. A first-frame-prompted tracking score must be reported as conditional-on-initialization and alongside the raw frame-0 target-placement error.

### Human agreement prerequisite

Double-label all 17 frames of two complete mirrored-seed blocks before choosing the observer threshold. For the five control-frame anchors `[0,4,8,12,16]`, compute x/y disagreement in latent-token units (`abs(delta_x_px)/16`, `abs(delta_y_px)/16`) and normalized image-diagonal disagreement. Proposed acceptance: human-human p95 absolute disagreement is **≤0.5 token cell per axis** (≤8 pixels at 832×480); exact agreement on visibility/count/identity is **≥95%** before adjudication. Adjudicate every disagreement that changes visible/ambiguous, instance count, identity, or center by >0.5 cell. If this prerequisite fails, repair the annotation instructions and repeat it; a noisy human reference cannot certify an observer.

## Metrics and proposed thresholds

| Gate/metric | Definition | Proposed first-pass criterion |
|---|---|---|
| **Observer coverage** | Of manually visible and identity-unique frames, fraction for which the observer emits a center accepted by its frozen rule. Report on all 17 frames and separately on the five intervention anchors. | At least **90% overall** on the held-out visible frames and at least **4/5 anchors in every clip**. If the object is absent/ambiguous/duplicated, observer must abstain; any confident accepted center on an ambiguous identity is a false accept. |
| **Observer center error** | For accepted centers, compare to blinded human box midpoint. Report x/y absolute error in grid cells and Euclidean error / image diagonal; also report median, p90/p95, and each per-frame error. | Median absolute error **≤0.5 cell per axis** and p90 **≤1 cell per axis** on held-out accepted frames. Do not drop rejected frames; coverage and error are reported together as a risk–coverage curve. |
| **Observer identity/false accept** | Compare accepted object ID/visible status to human labels; count duplicate detections, identity switches, accepted false positives on absent/ambiguous frames. | **Zero accepted wrong-identity/duplicate centers** at any of the five control anchors; zero confident accepts on human-absent or identity-ambiguous anchors. Flag any violation as observer-gate failure, even if mean center error is low. |
| **Stability cutoff** | For red threshold or model score, plot accepted-center error versus coverage for all candidate cutoffs on development data; choose and freeze one before holdout. | Do **not** inherit `0.65` as probability or presume it is calibrated. The held-out acceptance point must meet coverage and error criteria above. With only five held-out seed blocks, call this an empirical screening gate, not confidence calibration. |
| **Human outcome visibility** | Number of frames where target is visible and identity-unique / 17; count duplicates separately. | At least **16/17** frames visible and identity-unique for a clip to pass the strict demo guard; retain failed clips and charge missing/ambiguous frames the predeclared max center error rather than removing them. |
| **Trajectory error** | `ADE = mean_t(||c_t - target_t|| / sqrt(W^2+H^2))`; `FDE` is the same at frame 16. Missing, duplicate, or identity-ambiguous targets receive error 1.0. | Report both every clip and seed-level paired means. Also report target-box IoU, signed endpoint displacement, monotonicity over adjacent visible pairs only, and its valid-pair denominator. No dropping or compressing missing frames. |
| **Mirrored-path response** | For each seed, `(delta_x_right - delta_x_left) / 0.40`, where each `delta_x` is the output center's normalized horizontal change from frame 0 to 16 and ±0.20 are the target endpoint offsets. | Positive and closer to 1 than no-control is directionally correct. Also score absolute frame-0 placement separately; this relative response must not hide a bad starting position. |
| **Apparent-size guard** | Per-frame human box width and height relative to the commanded box dimensions; also report frame-0-relative median, CV, and extrema. | Proposed fixed-tabletop gate: median width and height ratio to the commanded box each in **[0.80,1.25]**, and no unexplained one-frame change >25%. Treat this as a quality guard, not part of the path score. |
| **Fixed-camera/background guard** | On static background landmarks outside the object, report per-point motion and coherent median translation/affine drift at frames 0,4,8,12,16; RAFT median flow outside the manual object mask is secondary. | If ≥4 reliable background landmarks exist, flag/fail a clip if coherent frame-0-to-16 displacement exceeds **1 token cell (16 px)** or coherent rotation/scale changes are visible. If the background has no trackable anchors, mark this guard *unscorable* rather than declaring camera stability from near-zero flow. |

These cutoffs are **proposed operating gates**, not established universal limits. Report raw values and plots so the thresholds are auditable; do not optimize thresholds against treatment outcomes.

## Small staged observer/evaluation design

| Stage | Samples and action | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **0 — synthetic mechanics** | Keep the existing paired 17-frame Kubric fixture and exact mask/projected-center truth. Confirm coordinate convention, missing penalty, mirrored sign, and manual-label package mechanics. Existing notes report these CPU checks pass. | Scorer arithmetic pass only. No machine observer or Wan claim follows. |
| **1 — human reference** | Double-label two complete mirrored-seed blocks (all 17 frames per clip); use the exact stream(s) consumed by the policy. Keep arm/path/seed hidden. Calculate agreement above and adjudicate errors. | Continue only if human agreement passes; otherwise clarify “visible silhouette,” partial/ambiguous identity, and box midpoint and relabel. |
| **2 — development selection** | Use **3 development seed blocks** with both mirrored paths. Compare the current red observer to SAM2.1 tiny/small proposals; optionally inspect OWLv2 only when mask proposals fail. Tune color thresholds / score cutoff only here. Use no target path to select the machine's object. | Freeze one observer, preprocessing, first-frame prompt protocol (if any), and cutoff. If choosing a prompted tracker, label its results “conditioned on frame-0 prompt.” |
| **3 — locked observer gate** | Evaluate on **5 new seed blocks** × both mirrored paths, with observer and cutoff frozen. Label all frames manually; independently double-label two held-out blocks to monitor annotation agreement. Score the exact step-4 `x0_hat` stream used for feedback; score step-14 separately only if it will also steer; label final clips separately. | Require the coverage, center-error, abstention, identity/duplicate, and label-agreement gates above. If it fails, reject the observer for feedback and keep it as a diagnostic only. No confidence probability claim from this small sample. |
| **4 — policy pilot then confirmation** | Only after observer pass, run matched base / fixed open-loop DiTraj / feedback / no-op or shuffled-residual controls, using same prompt, seed noise, and paired left/right target paths. Begin with **5 matched seed blocks** for feasibility screening. If promising, freeze the law and score **at least 10 new matched seed blocks** for a confirmatory claim; keep all paths, arms, frames, and checkpoints for one seed in one group. | Analyze paired treatment differences at the **seed-block** level (not per frame); use paired bootstrap or exact paired randomization, report intervals, and all failures. Claim an improvement only if path error improves and signed mirrored response moves in the right direction while visibility, identity, apparent size, and background guards do not regress. |

The 3/5/10 sequence keeps threshold tuning off the held-out observer sample and keeps observer validation separate from the eventual policy-effect claim. If compute only permits the five-seed pilot, label it exploratory; even five same-direction wins do not meet a conventional two-sided exact sign-test threshold (p=0.0625).

## Compute and licensing cautions

- Treat all vision models as **post-generation/offline tools first**. The T4 already serves Wan generation; official sources do not provide RAM/latency guarantees for SAM2, SAM3, TAPNext++, OWLv2, GroundingDINO, or RAFT at 832×480. The light-looking parameter count is not a peak-memory measurement. Unload Wan before running segmentation/tracking, process one clip at a time, and profile before considering an in-loop external observer.
- SAM2.1 is the lowest-friction mask proposal: official Apache-2.0 weights and code, small checkpoints, direct video propagation, but CUDA build/dependency friction. SAM3 is gated and custom-licensed. CoTracker3 is mostly CC-BY-NC. TAPNext++ is the clearest Apache-licensed point-track alternative. GroundingDINO code is Apache-2.0 and CPU-capable but its optional GPU setup compiles CUDA ops; OWLv2's model card explicitly says Apache-2.0. Verify every model/checkpoint and dataset artifact separately before reuse or redistribution.
- A post-generation segmenter can serve as annotation help or an independent observer check; it is not automatically a feasible latent-policy sensor. Closed-loop use must first prove that inference fits the active T4 memory/time budget and that inference occurs on the exact scheduler-converted predicted-clean output rather than a later/final-frame surrogate.
- RAFT measures apparent inter-frame flow, not camera pose. A homogeneous gray tabletop may not supply reliable background landmarks; record an unscorable background guard rather than inferring “locked camera” from uninformative flow.

## Exact remaining unknowns

- **Generated-sample observer accuracy:** no SAM2, TAPNext++, OWLv2, GroundingDINO, or RAFT observer has yet been run or human-scored on the M0 17-frame outputs in this review. The existing red-mask comparison of predicted-clean previews with final frames uses the same color proxy on both sides; it is a self-consistency check, not independent label accuracy or path adherence.
- **Human reference reliability:** the workspace's two blinded annotation packages still need independent labels and adjudication. Until the agreement threshold is measured, reported center error on generated clips is not calibrated to annotation noise.
- **The `0.65` red stability value:** its raw score definition, development risk–coverage curve, and held-out error/coverage are not established. It must not be interpreted as a 65% probability.
- **T4 feasibility:** official sources reviewed here do not give peak VRAM or latency for SAM2.1, TAPNext++, OWLv2, CoTracker3, GroundingDINO, or RAFT at 832×480 on a T4. Post-generation feasibility is plausible, but unmeasured; in-loop use is a separate and higher-risk question.
- **Diffusion-specific failure distribution:** it remains unknown how often this checkpoint produces hue changes, soft or multiple red regions, specular holes, identity swaps, partial disappearance, or background texture motion that breaks masks and point tracks. The red ball is a weakly textured and symmetric target, so success here will not validate future asymmetric-object orientation or 3D geometry measurement.
- **Background observability:** the current plain tabletop may have too few stable landmarks for a trustworthy camera-motion estimate. RAFT near-zero flow on a low-texture surface is not evidence of a fixed camera.
- **Rights of future use:** SAM3 uses a custom license and gated weights; CoTracker3 is predominantly noncommercial; individual datasets and checkpoints can have terms that differ from their source-code repositories. Any redistribution or non-research use needs a per-artifact license check.
