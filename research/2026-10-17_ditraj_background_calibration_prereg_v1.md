# DiTraj background metric calibration preregistration v1

**Status:** source/document protocol only. Calibration data have not been collected and no thresholds, packet, generated-output score, or Gate 1 result exist. This supplements, and does not replace, the [background-stability method decision](2026-10-17_ditraj_background_stability_protocol.md), the output contract, or any registered release gate.

## Gap crosswalk

The component plan requires background feature/flow stability as a scene guardrail. The output contract requires a separately frozen background measure before packet distribution. The October 17 method memo chose a static target-exclusion ROI, sparse forward/backward Lucas–Kanade (LK) motion, raw ROI appearance changes, and separate calibration, but leaves the control split, halo/coverage calibration, threshold derivation, held-out check, and exact provenance record unspecified. This preregistration fills those method gaps only. It does not change Gate 1's human-label thresholds, the six matched arms, the full-sequence design, or the requirement to score every decoded frame.

## Calibration set and control matrix

Use data separate from all six evaluation clips. Split by **scene/background**, not by adjacent frames or repeated encodings of the same scene: a development split is used to choose the mask/measurement settings and candidate thresholds; an untouched scene-level holdout checks those choices once. Keep a separate condition key; the measurement operator receives opaque calibration IDs until metric outputs and settings are frozen. Each sequence uses the same fixed camera, 832×480 source pixels, frame cadence and 17-frame/full-frame analysis expected downstream.

| Control | Ground-truth condition | Expected geometric LK response | Expected appearance response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Static repeat | Camera, scene, lighting, and target absent or stationary | Near the calibrated measurement-noise floor; adequate accepted-track coverage | Near repeat/export noise floor |
| Moving target and shadow | Fixed camera/background; target traverses the planned corridor with a known target-and-shadow mask | Static-region tracks remain near the static control after the halo excludes the target/shadow | Raw change falls as the calibrated halo captures target/shadow; residuals remain visible |
| Illumination/exposure only | Fixed camera and scene; controlled global and spatial light changes | No coherent translation should be inferred; report coverage loss if brightness changes break tracks | Luma/RGB deltas increase in the controlled direction and magnitude |
| Codec/compression only | Identical source frames passed through the actual export/decode path; add lossy round-trip variants only if the production path uses a video codec | Displacement should stay within the calibrated codec/no-motion envelope; any loss of coverage is recorded | Lossy encoding changes raw decoded pixels relative to the identical-frame control; do not assume a monotonic bitrate response. Identical PNG pixels remain identical regardless of PNG compression level |
| Known camera translation | Static textured background moved by a calibrated image-plane displacement; record signed ground truth in pixels per frame pair | Median signed flow follows the imposed direction and its magnitude increases with the imposed shift | Raw differences increase with misregistration |
| Local scene change | Camera fixed; a non-target background item or patch changes/moves | Local/tile flow rises without requiring a global camera-motion interpretation | Local RGB/luma change rises in affected tiles |

Include repeated controls over distinct development and holdout backgrounds. Before capture, record the smallest camera displacement that should count as material, and the largest illumination/codec variation considered acceptable. These are task tolerances, not values inferred from the evaluation clips. If the owner/task specification provides neither, the protocol can still report metrics and calibration curves but cannot make a binary “stable” decision.

## Mask and measurement settings

1. **Canonical pixels:** preserve the source export and SHA-256 it. Decode once with a pinned decoder to 832×480 RGB PNGs; do not resize, stabilize, denoise, color-correct, or re-encode after decoding. If generation already returns RGB frames, record that path and do not invent an MP4 step. Record codec/container, pixel format/chroma, timestamps/frame rate, encoder settings, decoder/version, color metadata, and the source and decoded-frame hashes whenever a video container is involved.
2. **Target/shadow exclusion:** on development controls only, use calibration ground-truth target and shadow masks to find the smallest dilation halo that excludes their full observed support while preserving adequate static-background ROI and known-translation recovery. Freeze that radius in source pixels. On holdout scenes, test the frozen halo without expansion. For future generated clips, form one static per-clip exclusion mask by taking the union of both independent blinded raters' valid target boxes over all 17 frames and applying the frozen halo. Record mask SHA-256 and retained/excluded pixel fractions. If target/shadow leakage or insufficient ROI remains, mark the clip unscoreable; never tune the halo on evaluation clips.
3. **Geometric signal:** detect Shi–Tomasi corners only inside the static ROI and track each adjacent pair forward and backward with the frozen pyramidal LK settings. Reject out-of-ROI endpoints, failed status, or forward/backward inconsistencies using a cutoff calibrated on development data. Preserve all 16 transition records per clip. Report signed median Δx/Δy, median and p95 displacement norm (px/frame-pair), candidate/accepted counts, accepted-track fraction, and spatial/tile coverage. No homography, global-motion subtraction, image registration, or stabilization is allowed in the primary measurement.
4. **Appearance signal:** on the same ROI and exact decoded pixels, retain per-channel raw RGB MAE/median/p95 divided by 255, and display-coded luma `Y' = 0.2126 R' + 0.7152 G' + 0.0722 B'` shifts in DN. Report adjacent-pair and frame-0-relative values. Do not normalize away gain/offset changes in the primary metric. Any gain/offset fit is diagnostic only and must be recorded separately. Summarize per clip as well as per transition; the 16 adjacent pairs are repeated observations, not 16 independent scenes.
5. **Coverage:** select the lowest accepted-track fraction and retained-ROI fraction at which known translations remain recoverable within the empirical stage/marker measurement uncertainty and static controls remain within the observed no-motion repeatability envelope. Freeze these coverage floors using development scenes and verify them on holdout. A small displacement score below either calibrated floor is *unscoreable*, never a stability pass. Report coverage and its spatial distribution for every pair.

For threshold fitting only, freeze the clip summaries as: geometric `G = p95_t(median_i(||d_it||_2))` in px/frame-pair; appearance `A = p95_t(mean_{ROI pixels, RGB channels}(|I_t-I_{t+1}|/255))`; and luma shift `L = p95_t(|median_ROI(Y'_{t+1})-median_ROI(Y'_t)|)` in DN. Keep frame-0-relative and per-channel values as diagnostics. Define coverage `C` as the minimum accepted-track fraction across the 16 transitions; also retain the complete per-transition and spatial coverage table. The report must retain raw frame-pair values so the summaries can be audited.

## Threshold selection and untouched holdout

Keep separate operating rules for geometry, appearance, and measurement coverage; do not collapse them into one score.

- For a metric with predeclared acceptable/no-motion controls and a predeclared material-change control, compute the protocol-memo clip summaries on development scenes. Define a candidate cutoff only if the worst acceptable-control score is strictly below the weakest material-change score; set the cutoff at the midpoint of that observed gap and record both endpoints. For geometry, the positive class is the predeclared minimum material camera translation. For appearance, the material-change control is the predeclared unacceptable appearance change; codec-only variation is an acceptable control only when that codec path is part of the actual evaluation pipeline.
- If the control ranges overlap, if no materiality/tolerance is specified, or if coverage cannot support the separation, **do not select a pass/fail cutoff**. Report the distributions and calibration curves descriptively and keep background classification unresolved.
- Freeze the exact numeric settings/thresholds, calibration split manifest, code/config hash, and decision before opening the untouched holdout. Do not refit on holdout. The held-out check must retain sufficient ROI/track coverage, keep all acceptable static/target-shadow/illumination/codec controls on the acceptable side of their frozen cutoff, and put the known material camera/appearance changes on the change side. Any overlap, missed positive, false alarm, or coverage failure invalidates that threshold; report the failure and require a new calibration amendment and new holdout scene before another attempt.
- This is a calibration/measurement validity check, not evidence of policy efficacy. The six output clips are too few to fit or revise a threshold, and their frames may not be used to tune the ROI, LK parameters, or cutoff.

The midpoint rule is a transparent small-pilot separation rule, not a statistical false-alarm guarantee. No numeric values or acceptable-change tolerances are supplied here. Do not claim a calibrated binary result until task tolerances are specified, calibration and holdout pass, and the versioned numeric preregistration is frozen before packet creation.

## Provenance checklist

For each calibration/holdout sequence, preserve: opaque calibration ID and split; separate condition/ground-truth record; scene/background ID; target/shadow mask source and hash; known translation Δx/Δy in pixels per pair; raw source hash; decoded-frame manifest/hash; dimensions, frame indices/timestamps/cadence and color encoding; container/codec/profile/pixel format/chroma and exact encoder/decoder versions/settings when applicable; frozen ROI/halo mask hash and retained-area fraction; OpenCV/Python versions; corner detector, LK, forward/backward and aggregation configuration hash; per-transition feature counts, coverage and flow/appearance values; derived per-clip summaries; calibration split and threshold endpoints; and final measurement output SHA-256. Keep condition labels and any later experiment arm/seed key outside measurement inputs until results and settings are locked.

## Stop conditions and release evidence

Stop threshold selection and retain descriptive metrics only if the holdout overlaps calibration classes, the frozen halo leaks target/shadow, ROI/track coverage is inadequate, brightness/compression causes unrecognized flow failure, codec/export differs by arm, hashes/dimensions/cadence do not match, or the minimum material-motion/appearance tolerances are unspecified. Do not repair this by stabilizing the frames or changing the cutoff on evaluation outputs.

Before the later 102-frame packet can be scored, require the existing two independent complete Gate 1 v5 human returns and their existing agreement/blind-review/truth stages; fresh verified no-paid admission for the full-schedule generation; six complete artifact-verified outputs at the registered 17 frames × 832×480; the immutable 102-row hash/dimension manifest; complete blinded output annotations; and this protocol's separate calibration/holdout evidence plus a versioned freeze of all numeric settings and thresholds. Keep whole-sequence generation and all-frame scoring. The Gate 1 blocker remains two missing independent complete v5 returns; this memo creates no packet and changes no gate.
