## Decision

Keep the current target narrow: one continuously visible object, fixed camera, 2D image-plane center path, Wan2.1 T2V 1.3B at 832×480, 17 frames, and 30 denoising steps. Use DiTraj as the first **open-loop actuator** because its official implementation explicitly patches the Wan2.1 T2V 1.3B Diffusers path, accepts normalized bounding-box keyframes, and pins Diffusers 0.33.1. This is an implementation-fit decision, not a novelty claim. The DiTraj repository is CC BY-NC 4.0; keep this adaptation in research use unless reuse terms are separately cleared. Its public examples and the literature do not establish that this 17-frame configuration runs on a T4.

The defensible LatentGate question is later and narrower: does an estimate of the generated object's **realized residual**, rather than the requested path, improve a subsequent denoising control action under matched compute? First show that the open-loop actuator moves the object in mirrored directions. Then test whether predicted-clean RGB or spatial DiT features predict held-out residuals. Only after the observer and actuator gates pass should one bounded feedback update be compared with exact no-op, frozen/static action, shuffled-state, and sign-reversed controls. A tracker or latent feature that only recovers the commanded path is not state feedback.

## Landscape and maturity findings

The completed report follows both the older U-Net trajectory-control line (TrailBlazer, Motion-Zero, FreeTraj and Tora) and the DiT/Wan line (DiTraj, ATI, Wan-Move, MagicMotion, FlashMotion, MotionStream, QWERTY and TrajLoc), then audits geometry/state methods (4DStreamCtrl, Geometry Forcing and VGGRPO). It included primary Chinese/Asian sources from Alibaba/Wan, ByteDance, East China Normal University, Fudan/MSRA, Tsinghua and the Beijing/Lenovo/HKUST/Tsinghua DiTraj collaboration.

Artifact and compatibility distinctions that affect our choice:

- **DiTraj**: public, inspectable code; exact Wan2.1 T2V 1.3B target; normalized box trajectory; foreground/background attention plus spatiotemporal positional manipulation. No training is needed. T4 memory and 17-frame support remain unmeasured. [Official repository](https://github.com/xduzhangjiayu/DiTraj), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21839).
- **Motion-Zero, FreeTraj and TrailBlazer**: important prior art for zero-shot or training-free trajectory control. Their earlier U-Net/attention mechanisms mean generic claims of training-free box-path control are already occupied; do not assume their code or interventions transfer to a Wan DiT unchanged.
- **Tora**: public trajectory-conditioned CogVideoX-5B path, but not a drop-in Wan 1.3B intervention; training and base-model terms matter.
- **ATI and Wan-Move/MoveBench**: useful Wan-native motion/track precedents, including explicit track visibility, but their released target is 14B I2V and their documented memory profile is around 40 GB, outside this T4 pilot.
- **TrajLoc**: per-object token/heatmap trajectory conditioning is a novelty collision, but the audited project says code is forthcoming and targets I2V/Larger backbones; no presently runnable artifact was verified.
- **FlexTraj/FlexBench**: a relevant point-trajectory and identity/segmentation collision. Its inspected setup spans Wan2.2-TI2V-5B and Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B; the minimal single-checkpoint T4 route and license were not verified.
- **MotionStream**: evidence that motion-conditioned causal students and streaming control are viable. Its reported performance is on H100, its teacher/student training is compute-heavy, and the audited code was not released. Its interactive commands are not feedback from the generated object's measured state.
- **QWERTY**: directly warns that naïve U-Net test-time guidance does not simply transfer to DiTs. Its latent-optimization result is on Wan2.2 TI2V-5B and reports about 40 GB peak memory for 49 frames, so it is not our first T4 route.
- **4DStreamCtrl, Geometry Forcing and VGGRPO**: establish strong adjacent precedents for 3D point tracks, geometry-aware diffusion features, latent geometry rewards, and causal students. They matter to the later novelty boundary, but they do not answer this small per-sample 2D T2V feedback question. Geometry Forcing also cautions that useful geometry is not guaranteed to emerge in vanilla diffusion features without explicit alignment.

These findings are from the report's paper-plus-artifact audit. A paper result does not imply a runnable repository; a public repository does not imply our backbone, frame count, memory, license, or metrics are compatible. The report's source panel was not exported locally, and its cutoff is 2026-10-01, so re-check upstream commits, terms, and compute if this project later adopts a new method.

## Measurement and falsification plan

Keep four score families separate: (1) path tracking, (2) visibility/count/identity, (3) background/camera stability, and (4) apparent size/shape. Use blinded per-frame human boxes and identity/visibility labels as reference; trackers, masks, color thresholds, or path-conditioned proposals are secondary observers, never the scoreboard. Preserve raw first-frame localization so start anchoring cannot hide an offset. The report also calls out the rolling-sphere trap: an object-center trajectory and a material surface-point track measure different things.

For known-truth synthetic fixtures, require ≤1 px center recovery on unobstructed frames and an explicit full penalty for a missing/wrong object before trusting model comparisons. For the current Gate 1 process screen, both raters must label all 34 frames in both mirrored clips. Score with `--expected-clips 2`; require pooled **and each clip-specific** visibility/count/identity agreement and center-disagreement thresholds to pass. This one mirrored pair is only a process screen; full reference reliability still requires a second independently rendered mirrored-seed block.

The completed report's open-loop proposal is six matched noise seeds, each with a mirrored path pair (12 controlled clips) plus matched prompt-only baseline clips. Its engineering screen requires valid identity/visibility in at least 15/17 frames per clip; at least 5/6 mirror pairs with the correct relative direction; median achieved/requested terminal separation ≥0.50; median disappearance-penalized path error ≤0.15 frame diagonals; median visibility ≥16/17; object-area ratio in [0.67, 1.50]; and background-motion guardrail no worse than +25% versus matched M0. These thresholds are project gates, not published standards. Retain all failures and unscoreable clips.

For later state observability, split by clip and seed, not frame. Compare residual prediction with path-only, frame-index/initial-center, and cross-seed/path shuffled controls. For later causal feedback, retain identical noise, denoising-call budget, and matched decodes in every arm; include open-loop, decode-matched no-op, frozen/static action, live residual, shuffled residual, and sign-reversed residual, plus a path that reverses direction. A true feedback result must outperform the fixed-schedule alternatives and fail under sign reversal; a gain from a static pulse is actuator tuning, not evidence that generated state helped.
